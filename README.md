# E-Commerce Sales Performance Dashboard 📊

An interactive **E-Commerce Sales Performance Dashboard** built using **Microsoft Power BI** to analyze sales, profit, orders, products, categories, cities, and sales channels.

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on transforming e-commerce sales data into an interactive business dashboard.

The dashboard helps understand overall business performance and identify important trends across:

- Sales
- Profit
- Orders
- Products
- Categories
- Cities
- Sales Channels
- Monthly Performance

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- Microsoft Power BI
- Power Query
- DAX
- Data Cleaning & Transformation
- Data Visualization

## 📊 Dashboard KPIs

- **Total Sales:** 26M
- **Total Profit:** 7.02M
- **Total Orders:** 1K
- **Average Order Value:** 25.79K

## 📈 Dashboard Features

### 1. Monthly Sales & Profit Trend
Analyzes monthly sales and profit performance throughout the year.

### 2. Sales by Category
Compares sales performance across different product categories.

### 3. Sales Trend by Product Category
Shows how different product categories perform over time.

### 4. Sales by Sales Channel
Compares sales generated through Online and Store channels.

### 5. Sales by City
Identifies cities contributing the most to overall sales.

### 6. Top 5 Products by Sales
Highlights the top-performing products based on sales.

### 7. Date Slicer
An interactive date slicer allows users to filter the complete dashboard by date.

## 💡 Key Insights

- Electronics is the highest-selling category.
- Laptop is the top product by sales.
- Jaipur is the highest-sales city.
- Store sales contribute slightly more than Online sales.
- Sales performance varies significantly across different months.

## 🎯 Project Objective

The main objective of this project was to practice **data cleaning, data modeling, DAX calculations, and interactive dashboard development in Power BI** while generating meaningful business insights from e-commerce data.
